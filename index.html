Family Trip

I was 5 or 6 years old when we had our first family trip. By the time we left the house it was already dark out.

We drove on 75 through downtown Dallas, the city lights getting further away into the country and the tall dark silhouettes of trees.

My younger siblings we're maybe 2-3 years old, they fell asleep way before I did.

I on the other hand was having a hard time falling asleep with how much the car was moving. Don't know for sure when I fell asleep.
