Family Trip

When we woke up, we had parked next to the beach. For me and my siblings it was the first time ever seeing the ocean.

My first instict was to run out in my pajamas. I hadn't realized it in the moment, but my siblings had run after me.

I ran straight into the cold water without a care in the world.

We spent maybe an hour playing in the water before we had to leave to check into our hotel.
